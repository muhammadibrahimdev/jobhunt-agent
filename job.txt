Job Summary

We are looking for a skilled Full Stack Developer who can develop, maintain, and optimize both front-end and back-end components of web applications. The ideal candidate should be comfortable working across the full development lifecycle, from designing user interfaces to managing databases and server-side logic.

Key Responsibilities

Design, develop, test, and maintain scalable web applications.

Build responsive and user-friendly front-end interfaces.

Develop and maintain server-side applications and APIs.

Integrate third-party services and APIs.

Optimize application performance, security, and scalability.

Collaborate with UI/UX designers, QA engineers, and project managers.

Troubleshoot, debug, and resolve technical issues.

Participate in code reviews and maintain coding standards.

Manage databases, data storage solutions, and application architecture.

Stay updated with industry trends, frameworks, and emerging technologies.

Requirements

Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field.

Experience 3+ years of Full Stack Development experience.

End Skills HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript. React.js, Next.js, Angular, or Vue.js. Responsive design and cross-browser compatibility.

Back-End Skills Node.js, Express.js, Laravel, .NET, Python Django, or similar frameworks.

RESTful API development and integration.

Database Skills MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, or SQL Server.

Database design and optimization.

Additional Technical Skills

Git and version control systems. Cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) preferred.

Understanding of CI/CD pipelines.

Authentication and security best practices.

Soft Skills Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.

Excellent communication skills.

Ability to work independently and within a team environment.

Time management and multitasking capabilities.

Preferred Qualifications

Experience with Docker and containerization.

Knowledge of microservices architecture.

Experience working in Agile/Scrum teams.

Familiarity with DevOps practices.
