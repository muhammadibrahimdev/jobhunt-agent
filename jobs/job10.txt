Job Title: Registered Nurse
Work Location: Hospital, on-site

Job Summary:
We are seeking a registered nurse to provide safe patient care in a clinical setting.

Responsibilities:
- Assess patients and monitor vital signs.
- Administer medications according to clinical instructions.
- Maintain accurate patient records.
- Assist physicians with examinations and treatment.
- Respond to emergencies and follow infection-control procedures.
- Educate patients and families about care plans.

Requirements:
- Recognized nursing qualification.
- Valid nursing registration or license required by the local regulator.
- Clinical placement or professional nursing experience.
- Knowledge of patient safety, medication administration, and infection control.
- Strong communication and teamwork skills.
- Ability to work rotating shifts.

Preferred:
- Experience in a hospital ward or emergency department.
