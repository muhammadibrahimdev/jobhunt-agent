Job Title: Sales Executive
Work Location: On-site

Job Summary:
We are hiring a sales executive to identify potential customers and sell business services.

Responsibilities:
- Make outbound calls and contact prospective customers.
- Meet weekly and monthly sales targets.
- Prepare quotations and follow up on leads.
- Maintain customer records in a CRM.
- Negotiate prices and close deals.
- Submit sales activity reports to management.

Requirements:
- Strong persuasion, negotiation, and communication skills.
- Confidence with cold calling and customer meetings.
- Ability to work toward sales targets.
- Previous sales experience is preferred.
- Willingness to work on-site and meet clients.

Preferred:
- CRM software experience and a background in business development.
